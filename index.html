Questions and answers

My visa portal only accepts files under 500 KB.

Scan the pages, or tap Shrink a big PDF. Choose Custom, type 500 KB, and upload when you see the ✓. If it can't get that small, try B&W or fewer pages. Portal wants JPG? On iPhone, Share as pictures (JPG) gives one picture per page; these don't follow the Custom limit, so check their size. Visa photo on iPhone: ID & visa photo, then your document, or Custom size with your file limit.

How do I shrink a PDF I already have?

Tap Shrink a big PDF in SlimScan, or share the PDF to SlimScan from Mail, Files, WhatsApp or your browser.

How small will my file be?

With Email (the default), about 75 KB a page: 20 pages in under 1.5 MB. Choose Small for the smallest file, Sharp for more detail, or Custom to set your own limit.

Why are some pages black and white?

In Auto, text pages become sharp black and white, which is far smaller, and colourful pages stay in colour. You can choose Colour, Grey or B&W for all pages instead.

My scan has a glare spot. Can I fix it? (iPhone)

Tap Edit under the page, then Remove glare, and take a second photo from a slightly different angle. SlimScan keeps the glare-free parts of both. Turning the flash off also helps.

Can I leave pages out, or crop them?

Tap a page to leave it out or add it back; the size updates instantly. To crop or rotate a page: on iPhone, tap Edit under the page; on Android, touch and hold the page. Touch and hold also moves or deletes a page.

What's free, and what does the unlock do?

Your first 3 PDFs are free, with a small watermark. Then Unlock SlimScan, pay once: no watermark, no limit, no subscription. The store shows the price in your currency. ID photos: framing and checking are free; saving and printing are part of the unlock.

I bought the unlock, but it asks me to pay again.

Tap Restore purchase on the unlock screen. Use the same Apple ID (iPhone) or Google account (Android) that you bought it with.

Does SlimScan work offline?

Yes. Scanning, shrinking and building PDFs happen on your phone, so it works in flight mode. Only buying or restoring the unlock needs a connection, and on Android the very first scan, which sets up Google's scanner.

Will my ID photo be accepted?

The checks are a guide, not a guarantee. Always confirm the current rules with the issuing office; some offices don't accept home prints. SlimScan is not affiliated with any government.

Does SlimScan collect my data?

On iPhone, no: nothing at all. On Android, SlimScan collects nothing either, but Google Play services sends Google technical diagnostics, never your documents. Details in the privacy policy.
