Frequently asked questions

How do I shrink a PDF I already have?
Open SlimScan and tap Shrink a Big PDF, or share the PDF to SlimScan directly from Mail, Files, or WhatsApp (tap Share → SlimScan). It imports the PDF as pages, ready to compress and re-send.
How small will my file actually be?
With the default EMAIL setting, SlimScan targets about 75 KB per page — so a 20-page document comes out under 1.5 MB. You can also choose TINY (40 KB/page) for the smallest possible file, or HI-Q (150 KB/page) when quality matters more than size.
Why do some pages look black-and-white and others stay in colour?
In AUTO mode, SlimScan keeps genuinely colourful pages (photos, coloured diagrams, letterheads) in colour, and converts plain text pages to sharp black-and-white — because black-and-white text compresses far smaller than colour JPEG without losing any readability. You can force COLOUR, GREY, or B&W for every page from the mode dial if you prefer.
My scan has a glare spot from a light or a window. Can I fix it?
Yes. Open the page in the editor and tap Remove Glare, then scan the same page again from a slightly different angle. SlimScan combines both photos and keeps whichever version is glare-free in each spot. Turning your camera flash off before scanning glossy paper also helps a lot.
Can I leave pages out of the PDF, or crop them?
Tap any page thumbnail to include or exclude it — the file size updates instantly. Touch and hold a page for crop, rotate, reorder, and delete options.
What's the free trial, and what does the $1.99 unlock?
Your first 3 exported PDFs are free (each carries a small watermark). After that, exporting is paused until you unlock SlimScan with a one-time $1.99 purchase — no subscription, ever. The unlock removes the watermark, removes the export limit, and works across your devices with the same Apple ID (and your family, via Family Sharing).
I bought the unlock before, but it's asking me to pay again.
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchase. Make sure you're signed into the same Apple ID you originally purchased with.
Does SlimScan work without an internet connection?
Yes. Scanning, editing, and building PDFs all happen entirely on your device, so SlimScan works fully offline — including in flight mode.
Does SlimScan collect any of my data?
No — nothing at all. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
