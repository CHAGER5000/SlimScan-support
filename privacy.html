Privacy Policy

Last updated 6 November 2026

No data collected by us No account No analytics No ads

The short version: SlimScan does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. It has no server, and your documents stay on your device. On Android only, Google's ML Kit software, which SlimScan uses, sends Google a small amount of technical diagnostic data; it is described in full below and contains none of your documents. Everything below explains exactly what happens.

What SlimScan does with your documents

Every scan, crop, colour adjustment, and PDF SlimScan builds happens entirely on your iPhone, iPad or Android phone. Your documents are never uploaded to us or to any third party. SlimScan has no server — there is nothing for your documents to be sent to.

Your documents leave your device only when you choose to send them — for example by tapping Email, Share, or Print. At that point, whatever app or service you picked (Mail, WhatsApp, AirPrint, etc.) handles the file under its own privacy policy. SlimScan itself has no part in that transfer beyond handing the file over.

Camera and Photos access

SlimScan asks for camera access to let you scan paper documents and take ID photos. When you import photos, you pick each one with the system photo picker (Apple's on iPhone and iPad, Android's on Android); SlimScan has no access to the rest of your photo library. Text recognition (Copy text), the hidden-data check and clean, and the ID & visa photo tools all run on your device. Images are not read, copied, or transmitted by SlimScan for any purpose other than the file you asked for.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

None of our own. SlimScan contains no analytics framework, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter that phones home, and no tracking pixels of any kind. We do not know how many people use SlimScan, what they scan, or how they use it. There is nothing in the app that could tell us. (On Android, Google's ML Kit sends Google the diagnostic data described in the Android section below; we never receive it.)

Purchases

On iPhone and iPad, the one-time unlock is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit, Apple's in-app purchase system. SlimScan never sees your payment details, billing address, or card information — Apple handles all of it under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was completed, with no personal information attached.

Data shared with third parties

We do not share, sell, or rent any data, because we do not collect any. The only third parties involved in using SlimScan are Apple (iPhone and iPad) and Google (Android), for app distribution, for processing the one payment if you buy the unlock and, on Android, for the ML Kit diagnostics described below.

SlimScan for Android

Everything above about your documents applies on Android too: scanning, cropping and PDF building happen on your phone, SlimScan has no server, and your documents leave the device only when you tap Email, Share, Print or Save.

Google's ML Kit

On Android, SlimScan uses Google's ML Kit for the document scanner and for on-device image features such as text recognition and photo checks. The document scanner is provided by Google Play services on your device. Your images are processed on the device and are not sent to Google or to us. To measure how ML Kit performs, it sends Google a small amount of technical diagnostic and usage data:

  • device information (manufacturer, model, Android version and build, available ML hardware)
  • app information (SlimScan's package name and version)
  • a device identifier and per-installation identifiers, which Google states are not intended to identify you as a person
  • performance and configuration details (for example how long a scan took, image format and resolution, input/output size, feature version, event type and error codes)

Google uses this data for diagnostics and usage analytics, encrypts it in transit (HTTPS), and states that it does not pass it to third parties. We, the developer, never receive it and cannot see it. It contains none of your documents, images or text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases on Android

The one-time unlock is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment under its own privacy policy; SlimScan never sees your payment details, name or email address. The app only asks Google Play whether this Google account owns the unlock, and remembers the answer on the device. That answer is excluded from Android backups, so it is checked again with Google Play on each new device.

Permissions

SlimScan for Android asks for camera permission for the ID & visa photo camera; it does not request storage, contacts or location permissions. The document scanner's camera is operated by Google's scanner. Photos are picked with Android's system photo picker, which only gives SlimScan the images you select, and PDFs are saved to your Downloads folder through Android's standard file system. The app has internet permission because Google software it includes needs it: ML Kit, to send the diagnostic data described above, and Google Play Billing, for the unlock. SlimScan's own code never connects to the internet and never uploads your files.

Children's privacy

SlimScan does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. The app is rated 4+ and can be used by anyone without creating an account or providing any personal information.

Your rights

Because SlimScan holds no personal data about you on any server, there is no account data to access, export, or delete on request — there simply isn't any. Anything SlimScan has ever "known" about your documents exists only on your own device, and deleting the app removes it completely.

ID & visa photo rules

The ID & Visa Photo feature uses typical published photo rules for each country and document. They were last compared on 11 Oct 2026 and are reviewed once a year. The checks in the app are only a guide: it is your responsibility to confirm the current rules with the issuing office before you apply, and SlimScan cannot guarantee that any photo will be accepted. Photos are prepared on your device and are never uploaded.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version of SlimScan adds an optional feature that involves a server — we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and describe the change plainly in that version's release notes.
