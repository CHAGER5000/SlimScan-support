Privacy Policy

Last updated 12 October 2026

  • iPhone: no data collected
  • Android: documents stay on the phone
  • No account
  • No ads

The short version: SlimScan has no server, so your documents never reach us. On iPhone and iPad, SlimScan collects no data at all. On Android, SlimScan itself collects nothing either, but Google's document scanner, part of Google Play services, sends Google technical diagnostics. It never sends your documents, images or text.

  • iPhone and iPad
  • Android
  • For everyone

iPhone and iPad

What SlimScan does with your documents

Every scan, crop, colour adjustment, and PDF SlimScan builds happens entirely on your iPhone or iPad. Your documents are never uploaded to us or to any third party. SlimScan has no server — there is nothing for your documents to be sent to.

Your documents leave your device only when you choose to send them — for example by tapping Email, Share, or Print. At that point, whatever app or service you picked (Mail, WhatsApp, AirPrint, etc.) handles the file under its own privacy policy. SlimScan itself has no part in that transfer beyond handing the file over.

Camera and Photos access

SlimScan asks for camera access to let you scan paper documents and take ID photos. When you import photos, you pick each one with Apple's own photo picker; SlimScan has no access to the rest of your photo library. Text recognition (Copy text), the hidden-data check and clean, and the ID & visa photo tools all run on your device. Images are not read, copied, or transmitted by SlimScan for any purpose other than the file you asked for.

Analytics, tracking, and advertising

None. SlimScan contains no analytics framework, no advertising SDK, no crash reporter that phones home, and no tracking pixels of any kind. We do not know how many people use SlimScan, what they scan, or how they use it. There is nothing in the app that could tell us.

Purchases

The one-time unlock ("Unlock SlimScan") is processed entirely by Apple through StoreKit, Apple's in-app purchase system. SlimScan never sees your payment details, billing address, or card information — Apple handles all of it under Apple's own privacy policy. We receive only a confirmation that a purchase was completed, with no personal information attached.

Data shared with third parties

We do not share, sell, or rent any data, because we do not collect any. The only third party involved in using SlimScan at all is Apple, solely for app distribution (App Store) and, if you buy the unlock, for processing that one payment.

Android

What SlimScan does with your documents

On Android too, scanning, cropping and building PDFs happen on your phone. SlimScan has no server, and your documents leave the phone only when you choose to send them, for example by tapping Email, Share, Print or Save.

Google's document scanner and diagnostics

As on every Android phone, Google Play services sends Google technical diagnostics. SlimScan scans with Google's ML Kit document scanner, which is part of Google Play services on your phone. The images you scan are processed on the phone and are not sent to Google or to us. To measure how the scanner performs, Google Play services sends Google:

  • device information (manufacturer, model, Android version and build)
  • app information (SlimScan's package name and version)
  • a device identifier and per-installation identifiers, which Google states are not meant to identify you as a person
  • performance details (for example how long a scan took, image size and format, error codes)

Google uses this data for diagnostics and usage statistics, encrypts it in transit, and states that it does not pass it to third parties. We never receive it and cannot see it. It never contains your documents, images or text. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure and Google's privacy policy.

Purchases

The one-time unlock ("Unlock SlimScan") is sold through Google Play Billing. Google Play processes the payment under its own privacy policy; SlimScan never sees your payment details, name or email address. The app only asks Google Play whether your Google account owns the unlock, and remembers the answer on the phone. That answer is left out of Android backups, so it is checked again with Google Play on each new phone.

Rating prompt

After your third PDF, SlimScan may ask Google Play once to show its rating sheet. Google Play decides whether it appears and handles any rating you give under its own policy.

Permissions

SlimScan for Android asks for no camera, storage, contacts or location permission. The camera is run by Google's scanner; photos are picked with Android's photo picker, which gives SlimScan only the images you choose; PDFs are saved to your Downloads folder with Android's standard file tools. Internet access is used only by Google Play services (the diagnostics above) and Google Play (purchases and the rating sheet). SlimScan itself makes no network requests.

Data shared with third parties

We do not share, sell, or rent any data, because we do not collect any. On Android, Google is involved for app distribution (Google Play), for the purchase (Google Play Billing) and for the scanner diagnostics described above.

For everyone

Children's privacy

SlimScan does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. The app can be used by anyone without creating an account or providing any personal information. On the App Store it is rated 4+.

Your rights

Because SlimScan holds no personal data about you on any server, there is no account data to access, export, or delete on request — there simply isn't any. Anything SlimScan has ever "known" about your documents exists only on your own device, and deleting the app removes it completely. On Android, the diagnostics described above are held by Google under Google's privacy policy; we have no access to them.

ID & visa photo rules (iPhone)

The ID & Visa Photo feature uses typical published photo rules for each country and document. They were last compared on 11 Oct 2026 and are reviewed once a year. The checks in the app are only a guide: it is your responsibility to confirm the current rules with the issuing office before you apply, and SlimScan cannot guarantee that any photo will be accepted. Photos are prepared on your device and are never uploaded.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes — for example if a future version of SlimScan adds an optional feature that involves a server — we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above, and describe the change plainly in that version's release notes.
